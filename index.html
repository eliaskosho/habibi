How it works

Three steps. Zero sweat, habibi.

  1. 1

    Someone trades $HABIBI

    Buy or sell on pons, it all counts. Volume is what fills the barrel.

  2. 2

    1% goes into the barrel, in Oil

    The creator tax on every trade is collected in Oil, the tokenized oil instrument on Robinhood Chain. Never in $HABIBI itself.

  3. 3

    The vault pours it out

    The pons V2 token vault claims the collected Oil and sends it to every holder, pro rata, in automatic rounds. Nothing to claim, nothing to press. This site only reads the results, and shows the cadence it measures.

Tokenomics

Habibinomics. The barrel, itemized.

1,000,000,000

Total supply

One billion $HABIBI, minted once. That is the whole family.

Fixed

No mint. Ever.

Supply is set at creation and cannot be changed.

1%

Creator tax

On every buy and every sell. Collected in Oil, never in $HABIBI.

Oil

What holders receive

USO, the tokenized United States Oil Fund instrument on Robinhood Chain. Habibi does not pay in memes. Habibi pays in Oil.

Auto

The cycle

The vault collects, then pours to all holders in rounds. Nobody presses a button. The live panel above shows the cadence measured from real pours, never a promised one.

Pro rata

Who gets it

Every holder, in proportion to their bag. Big bag, big pour. Small bag, still a pour.

Collection and distribution are handled natively by the pons V2 token vault. This site runs no distribution logic of its own; it only reads what happened on-chain.
